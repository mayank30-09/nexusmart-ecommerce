Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods are supported?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking via Razorpay, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

Can I cancel an order after placing it?

Yes, you can cancel any order directly from your Order Details page as long as it has not yet reached the dispatched/shipped stage.

How do I use a discount coupon?

Enter your valid coupon code (e.g. WELCOME10) on the Checkout Review page and click Apply to recalculate your subtotal instantly.
