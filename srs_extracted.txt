INTRODUCTION
1.1 Purpose
The purpose of this Software Requirements Specification (SRS) is to define the functional and non-functional requirements of the E-Commerce Website.

This document specifies the expected behavior, major features, users, interfaces, data requirements, business rules, constraints, and quality requirements of the system. It provides a common reference for the development team, testers, project guide, administrators, and future maintainers.

The requirements are expressed using clear and testable statements, primarily using the term “shall”, so that the implemented system can be verified against the specified requirements.
1.2 Scope
The E-Commerce Website is a web-based mini-project designed to allow customers to purchase products online.
The system shall provide customers with facilities to:
Create an account and log in.
Manage their profile and delivery addresses.
Browse products by category.
Search for products using keywords.
Filter products according to available criteria.
View detailed product information.
Add, remove, and update products in a shopping cart.
Review the cart and order details before checkout.
Make online payments through an integrated payment gateway.
Place orders after successful payment confirmation.
View order history and current order status.
Cancel orders before shipping.
Receive product recommendations based on customer activity.
The system shall also provide an administrator section for:
Adding, editing, and removing products.
Updating product details and stock.
Managing inventory.
Managing discount and coupon codes.
Viewing orders and updating order status.
The system shall also provide appropriate error handling for situations such as invalid login, failed payment, unavailable products, invalid coupons, and order-processing failures.
A simple AI-based recommendation feature shall suggest products based on customer activity. For new customers without sufficient activity, the system shall provide general recommendations.
1.3 Intended Audience
This SRS is intended for the following stakeholders:
Developers: To understand the required system behavior and functionality.
System Analysts: To analyze and maintain the requirements.
Testers: To verify that the developed system satisfies the specified requirements.
Project Guide/Faculty: To review the project requirements and scope.
Administrators: To understand the functions available for managing the system.
Future Maintainers: To understand the system requirements when making corrections or modifications.
1.4 Definitions and Abbreviations
OVERALL DESCRIPTION
2.1 Product Perspective
The E-Commerce Website is a web-based software system that provides an online platform for browsing and purchasing products.
At a high level, the system consists of:
Customer Interface → Application System → Database
The customer interacts with the system through web pages for registration, login, product browsing, searching, filtering, cart management, checkout, payment, and order management.
The administrator interacts with separate administrative functions for product management, inventory management, discount management, and order management.
The system shall interact with an external Payment Gateway for online payment processing.
The system shall also contain a simple AI Recommendation Component that uses available customer activity, such as viewed, searched, and previously purchased products, to provide product recommendations.
The exact programming languages, frameworks, database system, and other implementation technologies are not fixed in this SRS. Specific implementation technologies will be finalized during the implementation phase.
2.2 Product Functions
The major functions of the E-Commerce Website are:
Customer registration.
Customer authentication and login.
Customer profile management.
Delivery address management.
Product browsing by category.
Product keyword search.
Product filtering.
Product detail viewing.
Shopping cart management.
Cart total calculation and updating.
Checkout and order review.
Online payment.
Payment confirmation and order creation.
Unique order identification.
Order history and status tracking.
Order cancellation before shipping.
AI-based product recommendations.
Product and inventory management by administrator.
Discount and coupon management.
Order management by administrator.
Error handling and appropriate error messages.
2.3 User Classes and Characteristics

2.3.1 Customer
A customer is a registered user who uses the website to browse and purchase products.
The customer shall be able to:
Register an account.
Log in securely.
Manage profile information.
Manage delivery addresses.
Browse available products.
Search and filter products.
View product details.
Add products to the cart.
Modify cart quantities.
Remove products from the cart.
Review order details.
Make online payments.
View order history and status.
Cancel eligible orders.
View product recommendations.
Customers are expected to have basic knowledge of using a web browser and online shopping interfaces.
2.3.2 Administrator
An administrator is an authorized user responsible for managing the operational data of the website.
The administrator shall be able to:
Add, edit, and remove products.
Update product information.
Update product stock.
Manage discount and coupon codes.
View customer orders.
Update order status.
Perform authorized administrative operations.
Administrator access shall be restricted to authorized users.
2.4 Operating Environment
The system shall operate as a web-based application.
The expected operating environment includes:
Desktop and laptop computers.
Smartphones and tablets.
Common modern web browsers.
Internet/network connectivity.
A web application environment consisting of application processing and data storage.
The system shall provide a responsive layout suitable for common desktop and mobile browsers.
Specific operating-system, programming-language, framework, and database requirements are not fixed at the SRS stage.
2.5 Constraints
The development and operation of the E-Commerce Website are subject to the following constraints:
The project is developed by a small student team.
Development must be completed within the semester/project schedule.
Available college or personal computing resources shall be used.
The project shall use a web-based frontend, backend, and database approach.
Online payment depends on an available payment gateway or suitable sandbox/test service.
Internet connectivity is required for online access and payment-gateway communication.
Administrative functionality shall be restricted to authorized administrators.
2.6 Assumptions and Dependencies
The following assumptions and dependencies apply to the system:
Customers have access to an internet connection while using the website.
The external payment gateway is available when online payments are attempted.
The payment gateway provides sufficient confirmation information for determining payment status.
Product, stock, customer, and order information required by the system is available in the system database.
The recommendation feature has access to available customer activity data.
New customers may not have sufficient activity data for personalized recommendations.
Specific implementation technologies will be finalized during the implementation phase.
The target of approximately 100 concurrent users applies to the initial mini-project and demonstration/testing scope.
FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Functional requirements define the operations that the E-Commerce Website shall perform. Each requirement is assigned a unique identifier for traceability and verification.
3.1 Customer Management
FR-01 — Customer Registration
The system shall allow customers to create an account using:
Name
Email address
Mobile number
Password
The system shall store the required customer account information for subsequent authentication and account management.
FR-02 — Customer Login
The system shall allow registered customers to authenticate using their email address and password.
The system shall reject invalid login credentials and display an appropriate error message.
FR-03 — Customer Profile and Address Management
The system shall allow authenticated customers to:
View and manage their profile information.
Add delivery addresses.
Update delivery addresses.
Manage their available delivery addresses.
Customers shall be able to use an appropriate delivery address during checkout.
3.2 Product Management
FR-04 — Product Browsing
The system shall allow customers to browse available products by category.
The system shall display products that are available for browsing according to their current product information and availability.
FR-05 — Product Search
The system shall allow customers to search for products using keywords.
The system shall display products matching the search criteria.
FR-06 — Product Filtering
The system shall allow customers to filter products using available product attributes, including:
Category
Price range
Availability
The system shall display products matching the selected filter conditions.
FR-07 — Product Details
The system shall display relevant product details, including:
Product name
Product images
Product description
Price
Category
Stock status
Applicable discount
The displayed product information shall reflect the current information available in the system.
3.3 Search and Filtering
Product search and filtering shall support customers in locating suitable products efficiently.
The system shall:
Accept keyword-based product searches.
Apply selected category filters.
Apply selected price-range filters.
Apply availability filters.
Display the resulting matching products.
Allow customers to continue browsing after performing a search or filter operation.
The normal response time for product browsing and search operations shall comply with NFR-01.
3.4 Shopping Cart
FR-08 — Cart Management
The system shall allow authenticated customers to:
Add products to the cart.
Remove products from the cart.
Update product quantities in the cart.
View the products currently added to the cart.
The system shall maintain the selected products and quantities as part of the customer's cart.
FR-09 — Cart Total Update
The system shall calculate and display the current cart total.
The system shall update the cart total when:
Product quantities are changed.
Products are added or removed.
Applicable discounts change.
The system shall ensure that the displayed total reflects the current cart contents and applicable discounts.
3.5 Checkout and Payment
FR-10 — Checkout
The system shall allow a customer to proceed to checkout after selecting products.
During checkout, the system shall allow the customer to:
Select a delivery address.
Review selected products.
Review product quantities.
Review applicable discounts.
Review the total amount.
Confirm the order details before payment.
The system shall verify current product availability before allowing the order to proceed.
FR-11 — Online Payment
The system shall allow customers to make online payments through an integrated external payment gateway.
The system shall receive and process the payment status returned by the payment gateway.
The system shall not directly store customer card details.
FR-12 — Order Creation
The system shall create a successful order only after receiving the required confirmation that the payment has been successfully completed.
If payment fails, the system shall not create a successful completed order for that failed payment.
The system shall support payment retry without creating duplicate successful orders.
FR-13 — Unique Order Number
The system shall assign a unique order number to every successfully created order.
The order number shall be used to identify the order during order history and order-status operations.
3.6 Order Management
FR-14 — Order History and Status
The system shall allow authenticated customers to view:
Their previous orders.
Current order status.
Relevant order information.
Customers shall only be able to access their own order information.
FR-15 — Order Cancellation
The system shall allow customers to cancel an order only when the order has not yet been shipped.
The system shall prevent cancellation after the order has reached the shipping stage.
3.7 AI-Based Recommendation
FR-16 — Product Recommendations
The system shall provide product recommendations based on available customer activity.
The recommendation feature may consider:
Products viewed by the customer.
Products searched by the customer.
Products previously purchased by the customer.
For new customers with insufficient activity data, the system shall provide general product recommendations.
The recommendation feature shall display suggested products to the customer.
3.8 Inventory Management
FR-18 — Inventory/Stock Control
The system shall maintain the available stock information for products.
The system shall prevent a customer from ordering a quantity greater than the available stock.
The system shall verify product availability before order completion.
The administrator shall be able to update product stock.
3.9 Discount Management
FR-19 — Discount/Coupon Management
The system shall allow authorized administrators to:
Create discount or coupon codes.
Update discount or coupon codes.
Remove discount or coupon codes.
Define validity periods for applicable coupons.
The system shall check coupon validity before applying a discount.
Invalid or expired coupons shall not reduce the order amount.
3.10 Administrator Management
FR-17 — Admin Product Management
The system shall allow authorized administrators to:
Add products.
Edit products.
Remove products.
Update product name.
Update product description.
Update product category.
Update product price.
Update product stock information.
FR-20 — Admin Order Management
The system shall allow authorized administrators to:
View customer orders.
Review order information.
Update order status.
Administrative functions shall only be accessible to authorized administrators.
3.11 Error Handling
FR-21 — Error Messages
The system shall provide appropriate error messages for conditions including:
Invalid login credentials.
Failed online payment.
Unavailable products.
Invalid or expired coupons.
Requested quantity greater than available stock.
Order-processing failures.
The system shall prevent duplicate successful orders when payment or order processing fails.
NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Non-functional requirements specify the quality attributes and operational constraints of the E-Commerce Website. They complement the functional requirements by defining how the system should perform. This separation between what the system does and how well it does it is standard SRS practice.
4.1 Performance
The system shall provide acceptable response time for normal customer operations.
For the initial mini-project, product browsing, product search, and cart-related operations shall normally respond within 3 seconds under the intended operating conditions.
Performance shall be considered in the context of the defined mini-project scope rather than enterprise-scale deployment.
4.2 Scalability
The initial system shall support approximately 100 concurrent users for demonstration and testing.
This value represents the intended initial project capacity and does not represent a requirement for commercial-scale deployment.
4.3 Security
The system shall protect customer account information from unauthorized access.
The system shall:
Protect passwords from plain-text storage.
Require authentication for personal account information.
Restrict customers to their own account and order information.
Restrict administrative functionality to authorized administrators.
4.4 Payment Security
Payment processing shall be performed through an integrated payment gateway.
The system shall not directly store customer card details.
The system may store necessary payment-related information such as payment status and transaction reference as required for order processing.
4.5 Usability
The system shall provide a simple and understandable user interface.
The interface shall support common shopping activities such as:
Browsing products.
Searching products.
Filtering products.
Adding products to the cart.
Reviewing the cart.
Completing checkout.
4.6 Accessibility
The system shall provide basic accessibility support.
Important interface elements shall have:
Readable text.
Clear labels.
Understandable controls.
Basic keyboard accessibility.
4.7 Reliability
The system shall handle payment and order-processing failures without creating duplicate successful orders.
The system shall also verify relevant product availability before completing an order.
4.8 Compatibility
The system shall provide a responsive layout that can be used on common:
Desktop browsers.
Laptop browsers.
Mobile browsers.
Tablet browsers.
4.9 Backup and Recovery
Important customer, product, and order data shall be backed up regularly.
The backup approach and recovery procedure shall be finalized according to the implementation environment available to the student project.
4.10 Maintainability
The application code shall be organized into separate modules so that individual parts of the system can be maintained or modified without unnecessarily affecting unrelated functionality.

The E-Commerce Website shall provide user interfaces appropriate for both customers and administrators.
Customer Interface
Important customer screens shall include:
Registration screen.
Login screen.
Product catalogue.
Product search and filtering interface.
Product details screen.
Shopping cart.
Checkout screen.
Payment interface.
Order history and order-status screen.
Customer profile.
Delivery-address management.
The interface shall use clear labels and understandable navigation.

Administrator Interface
Important administrator screens shall include:
Product management.
Inventory/stock management.
Discount/coupon management.
Order management.
Order-status management.
Administrative screens shall only be accessible to authorized administrators.
5.2 Hardware Interface
The system does not require specialized hardware.
The system shall be usable through:
Desktop computers.
Laptop computers.
Smartphones.
Tablets.
Keyboard and display devices.
Standard internet/network connectivity.
5.3 Software Interface
The system shall interact with the following software components or external services:
Specific frameworks, programming languages, and database products are not defined in this SRS.
5.4 Communication Interface
The system shall use internet/network communication for:
Communication between the web browser and application system.
Access to the web application.
Communication with the external payment gateway.
Payment-related communication shall depend on the interface and services provided by the selected payment gateway.
SYSTEM DATA REQUIREMENTS
The system shall handle the following major categories of data.
The system shall maintain data required for the operation of customer management, product management, cart management, payment, orders, inventory, discounts, and recommendations.
The SRS does not define a detailed database schema or normalization design because those details belong to the design and implementation phase.
BUSINESS RULES
The following business rules define important conditions that shall be followed by the system.
BR-01 — Customer Authentication
A customer shall authenticate before accessing personal account information and order information.
BR-02 — Stock Availability
A customer shall not be allowed to order more than the available stock of a product.
BR-03 — Payment Confirmation
A successful order shall require the required confirmation of successful payment.
BR-04 — Order Cancellation
Order cancellation shall be allowed only before the order is shipped.
BR-05 — Coupon Validity
Invalid or expired coupons shall not reduce the order amount.
BR-06 — Customer Data Access
A customer shall only be able to access their own account and order information.
BR-07 — Administrator Authorization
Only authorized administrators shall be allowed to access administrative functions.
BR-08 — Card Information
The website shall not directly store customer card details. Payment processing shall be handled through the external payment gateway.
CONSTRAINTS
The following constraints are applicable to the E-Commerce Website.
AI-BASED RECOMMENDATION FEATURE
The E-Commerce Website shall provide a simple AI-based recommendation feature to help customers discover products relevant to their activity on the website.
9.1 Purpose
The purpose of the recommendation feature is to suggest products that may be relevant to a customer's interests and previous activity.
The feature is intended to provide simple product recommendations within the scope of the mini-project.
9.2 Input
The recommendation feature may use available customer activity such as:
Products viewed by the customer.
Products searched by the customer.
Products previously purchased by the customer.
The available activity shall be used as input for generating suitable product recommendations.
9.3 Recommendations for New Customers
A new customer may not have sufficient activity information for personalized recommendations.
In such cases, the system shall display general product recommendations.
These recommendations may be based on generally relevant or popular products available in the system.
No specific machine-learning algorithm or implementation technology is mandated by this SRS.
9.4 Output
The recommendation feature shall display suggested products to the customer.
The output shall consist of product recommendations that the customer can view and potentially select for further browsing.
The recommendation feature shall not alter the customer's cart or place an order automatically.
ERROR HANDLING
The system shall provide clear and appropriate responses when an operation cannot be completed.
Error messages shall be understandable to the user and shall indicate, where appropriate, that corrective action is required.
